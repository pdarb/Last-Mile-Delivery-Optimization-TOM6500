Ask about constraints (time windows, priority stops) or ask the AI to explain the route. Powered by Claude.
Methodology: Synthetic delivery data for a simulated Seattle-metro operator ("MetroSwift"). AI Layer 1 — nearest-neighbor construction + 2-opt local search (combinatorial optimization) builds the efficient route. AI Layer 2 — an LLM (Claude) interprets natural-language dispatcher constraints and explains route logic in plain English. Cost assumptions: $0.72/km blended fuel+labor rate, 35 km/h avg urban speed, 12-truck fleet, 260 working days/year.
